# Black Tithe 2 — Campaign State

## Design rules
- 2D campaign.
- Campaign goal: build toward rebellion against the Synod.
- Start with one manor; progression must be slow and non-repetitive.
- Tithe evolves in kind and political/supernatural significance, not only quantity.
- Tactical raids are a major system and should be improved.
- Persistent characters, conversations, complex NPC motives, plot, side characters, and genuine plot twists are required.
- Player must not become overwhelmingly powerful too quickly.
- Simulation-first; canonical death; world does not scale to player level; real randomized rolls; retreat can be success.
- Use-based progression. Power must have meaningful costs, limits, counters, and consequences.

## Nemesis system
- At campaign creation, one hidden Arch-Nemesis is randomly generated.
- The Arch-Nemesis progresses independently of the player: relationships, rank, resources, injuries, ambitions, victories and failures can change off-screen.
- An NPC who kills a player character can become a persistent Nemesis.
- On player-character death, play continues as another character in the same persistent world.
- Nemeses can therefore survive across player characters and carry history forward.

## Player Character

- Age: 25
- Gender: male
- Defining trait: power-starving; strongly motivated to acquire authority and power.

## Greywake Manor
- Starting population: 73 souls.

- Jon inherited Greywake after his uncle Lord Edric Vale died 12 days before campaign start.
- Official cause of Edric's death: fever.
- Edric's body was sealed in a lead-lined coffin; reason currently unknown.
- First Tithe is due in 28 days.
- The black Synod Tithe envelope remains sealed as of the current state.

## Persistent Characters

### Mara Venn
- Age: 41.
- Greywake's severe steward.
- Effectively ran the manor for years before Jon inherited it.
- Knew Lord Edric well.
- Has an established dislike/conflict with Brother Caldus.
- Told Jon that Edric opened his Tithe alone each year and then spent weeks frightened.

### Brother Caldus
- Young Synod priest assigned to Greywake.
- Charming; loyalty is not yet established as genuine.
- Knew Lord Edric.
- Entered Edric's study without knocking and wanted to be present when Jon opened the Tithe.
- Has an established conflict with Mara Venn.

### Tomas Rook
- Scarred huntsman.
- Knew Lord Edric.
- Has not spoken to Brother Caldus since Edric's funeral.
- At the current moment he is involved in a disturbance/argument outside in Greywake's courtyard.

### Lord Edric Vale
- Jon's deceased uncle and predecessor at Greywake.
- Died 12 days before Day 1, officially of fever.
- Body sealed in a lead-lined coffin.
- Was frightened by his annual Tithe.
- His death and behavior surrounding the Tithe are unresolved mysteries.

- Their identity, location, capabilities, ambitions and current actions remain hidden from the player until discovered naturally.
