## Resolution and progression rules
- Uncertain checks require a genuine randomized d20. Do not invent or narrate fake rolls.
- Difficulty comes from the world and does not scale to Jon.
- Retreat or non-intervention can be success.
- No instant expertise and no multi-week/month training skips.
- Progression is use-based: meaningful practice, instruction, field use, experimentation, and informative failure can improve capability.
- Magic obeys concrete constraints: source, quantity, state, receiver, coupling, capacity, direction, loss, and consequence.

### Interconnected level system
Progression has six interacting layers:
1. **Attributes** — Strength, Agility, Endurance, Intellect, Perception, Will, Presence. Change very slowly through sustained experience, injury, deprivation, age, etc.
2. **Skills** — rated 0–10. Rough interpretation: 0 untrained, 2 practiced, 4 professionally competent, 6 expert, 8 exceptional, 10 realistic upper edge of mastery.
3. **Knowledge** — distinct from skill; subject knowledge is learned through teachers, books, observation, experimentation, and experience.
4. **Relationships** — persistent trust, respect, fear, loyalty, resentment, obligations, and personal history affect cooperation and information.
5. **Reputation** — different groups may know Jon differently; reputation is not a single universal score.
6. **Estate Development** — Greywake capabilities include Agriculture, Infrastructure, Education, Military, Administration, Economy, Health, Intelligence, and Political Influence.

- Typical uncertain resolution: d20 + relevant Attribute + Skill + justified Knowledge/situational modifiers versus world-based difficulty.
- Relevant secondary skills may provide small synergy bonuses, never unlimited stacking.
- Informative failures can contribute to learning.
- NPCs progress under the same underlying logic.
- Estate systems interact causally: e.g. agricultural efficiency can free labor; education can improve future administration and military leadership; military commitments can reduce harvest labor.
- Estate capabilities represent actual people/resources/institutions and can decline if those foundations are lost.
- Important thresholds may be surfaced as e.g. "Skill Developed — Livestock Assessment I" rather than fractional XP spam.
- Jon's previous actions count only where he genuinely practiced or learned something; no retroactive invented expertise.

## Oren Vey / Edric investigation
Oren and Edric secretly investigated the Tithe system.

### Ledger 1 findings
- Tithe demands vary significantly between similarly sized holdings.
- A village resisting a Synod appointment received a harsher Tithe the next year.
- Another holding received unusually light demands after its lord supplied soldiers.
- Evidence suggests Tithe demands are partly economic and partly used to reward obedience or punish resistance.

### Ledger 2 findings
- Some collected grain disappears from official inventories.
- Livestock is redirected.
- Coin is recorded under different offices.
- Repeated unexplained notation: **BLACK ALLOCATION**.
- Black Allocation appears beside portions of Tithes from several settlements, including Greywake.
- Edric wrote: **"Find where the Black Allocation goes."**
- Edric also wrote: **"Caldus does not know."**
- Oren wrote: **"If they discover we copied these, neither of us survives."**

### Destruction of originals
- Jon spent hours copying the most important information into his personal cipher notebook.
- He then burned both ledgers and the loose correspondence page by page and destroyed readable remains.
- Nobody witnessed the burning.
- Anything Jon failed to copy is permanently lost.
- Jon's encoded notebook is the **sole known surviving written record** of the investigation.
